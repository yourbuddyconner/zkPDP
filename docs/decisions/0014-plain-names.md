# 0014: Plain names, no coined names

Status: accepted. Date: 2026-11-04.
Proposed by: human, as a standing preference from earlier work.
Recorded by: assistant.

## Decision

The project is `zk-pdp`, the human's name for it. The backends are "Cedar in a
zkVM" and "Cedar compiled to a circuit", or "the zkVM backend" and "the circuit
backend". The envelope is "the proof-carrying AuthZEN response". No product
names, no acronyms invented here.

## Reasoning

A coined name hides what a thing is and invites the reader to think it is more
than it is. Reviewers notice.

## Consequences

- The paper title is descriptive. The working title in `paper/outline.md` follows this.
