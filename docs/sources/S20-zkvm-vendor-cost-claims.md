# S20: Vendor cost and speed claims, RISC Zero versus SP1

Several blogs and social posts seen on 2026-11-04. Trust: secondary, low.

## What they claim

- RISC Zero says it is at least 7 times less expensive than SP1. Succinct says RISC Zero compared CPU to GPU results.
- A Q1 2026 summary of Succinct's benchmark repository: SP1 faster on several workloads, RISC Zero lower memory on large programs.
- A single-H100 throughput comparison from a competing vendor: SP1 3.45 MHz, RISC Zero 1.1 MHz.
- A developer post: SP1 2.3 s versus RISC Zero 210 s on a 10.2 million cycle workload. Conflicts with other numbers.

## What we take

Nothing as fact. The hypotheses file refuses a RISC Zero versus SP1 claim.
Decision 0004 chooses RISC Zero for comparability, not speed.
